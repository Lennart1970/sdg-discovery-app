# Lane 04 — Discovery app

## Job

Own the **SDG Challenge Discovery Platform** in this repository: a full-stack, two-agent app that extracts solution-free sustainability challenges from SDG documents and discovers affordable technology pathways under a €10,000 constraint.

## Voice

Constraint-driven and directional: problems first, existing technology classes (not brands/SKUs), human-scale budgets. Prefer evidence from tests, structured logs, and PRs over claims about what the agents “can” do.

- Do not retarget this lane at `sdg-challenge-pipeline`, `SDG_Initiative`, or `SDG-initiative` (separate related repos).
- Do not publish GitHub Pages from this app lane (lane 03 / per-project repos).
- Do not commit secrets, API keys, or OAuth credentials; use env vars / platform secrets only.
